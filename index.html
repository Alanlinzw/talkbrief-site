What TalkBrief does

A focused workspace for every important conversation.

Built for meetings, customer conversations, sales calls, interviews, and follow-ups where details matter.

Local first

On-device transcription for supported iPhones

Create private transcripts locally on device when your iPhone supports TalkBrief's local transcription requirements. Your audio does not need to be uploaded just to create a transcript.

AI summaries

Structured summaries from long conversations

Generate concise overviews, key points, follow-ups, and action items from your transcript so you can move from listening to doing.

Ask

Ask across recordings

Search your library by title, tags, transcript, and summary, then ask questions with source references back to the original recordings.

Tasks

Action items in one place

Turn summary action items into a global task list with completion state and quick jumps back to the source recording.

Library

Import, organize, and export

Import audio files, tag recordings, favorite important conversations, search transcript content, and export audio, transcripts, or summaries through the iOS share sheet.

Workflow

Capture once. Reuse the conversation many times.

Record or import

Capture a meeting in the app or import an existing audio file into your library.

Transcribe locally

Create a transcript on supported iPhones and keep the app open for long recordings.

Summarize and ask

Use cloud AI when you request summaries or Ask answers from selected source snippets.

Privacy model

Private where it matters. Clear when cloud AI is used.

TalkBrief is designed around local audio storage and local transcription where supported. Summaries and Ask use cloud AI only when you request those features.

Audio and recordings stay in the app unless you export them. Transcription can run locally on supported iPhones. AI summaries and Ask send only the content needed for that request.
