Your privacy, explained.

Local recordings and transcription

Recordings, imported audio or video, transcripts, summaries, tags, favorites, generated tasks, and task completion state are stored in the app's local container on your device unless you choose to export, share, enable iCloud Sync, or request cloud transcription. Transcription runs locally on device when your iPhone supports TalkBrief's local transcription requirements.

Speaker identification

With Apple On-Device transcription, optional speaker identification analyzes audio locally on your device. The first time you use it, TalkBrief downloads speaker models from Hugging Face; no recording audio is uploaded for this local mode. Speaker labels are saved with the recording and sync to your private iCloud when iCloud Sync is enabled.

High-Accuracy Cloud transcription

Pro users may explicitly choose High-Accuracy Cloud transcription. TalkBrief uploads the selected recording to TalkBrief Cloud, using Cloudflare infrastructure and currently Google Cloud Speech-to-Text with temporary Google Cloud Storage, to produce a transcript, word timings, and speaker labels. Temporary audio is deleted from TalkBrief Cloud after processing or failure cleanup, and the provider-uploaded file is deleted after transcription. The normalized transcription result is deleted from TalkBrief Cloud after the app confirms download. TalkBrief keeps only minimal task, entitlement, and usage metadata needed for quota, reliability, and abuse prevention; it does not keep a permanent server-side audio archive.

Cloud AI features

Summaries, Ask, and AI translation use cloud AI only when you request those features. TalkBrief sends the relevant transcript, summary, custom template instructions, question, selected source snippets, and output language to TalkBrief’s Cloudflare service and its AI provider (currently DeepSeek for summaries and Ask) so they can generate the requested response.

Usage limits and subscriptions

TalkBrief uses Apple's in-app purchase system for subscriptions. Apple processes purchase and payment information. To unlock Pro features and enforce free monthly AI limits, TalkBrief may send an App Store transaction ID, App Store environment, and feature usage information to the TalkBrief Cloudflare service. The service stores entitlement status, cloud transcription task state, and monthly usage in D1.

iCloud Sync

If you turn on iCloud Sync, TalkBrief syncs recordings, audio files, transcripts, summaries, tags, favorites, generated tasks, and task completion state to your private iCloud using the Apple Account signed in to iCloud on this device. You can turn off iCloud Sync and delete TalkBrief cloud data from Profile. Local recordings remain on your device unless you delete them.

TalkBrief Web

TalkBrief Web authenticates with Apple CloudKit and accesses your private iCloud database to display synced recordings and documents. Audio is requested when you choose to play a recording. Downloads and copied text are handled by your browser and the destination you choose.

iCloud sharing

When you choose Share with TalkBrief User, TalkBrief creates a read-only iCloud share for the selected brief. The share can include the summary, action items, and transcript if you turn on Include transcript. Audio files are not included. Anyone with the iCloud share link may be able to accept the share and view the shared brief in TalkBrief, depending on Apple's iCloud sharing flow. You can stop sharing through the iCloud sharing controls.

Reminders and exports

When you choose Add to Reminders, TalkBrief adds selected action items to Apple Reminders with the task title, notes, owner, due date, source recording title, and a TalkBrief deep link. This uses the device Reminders permission and does not send those tasks to TalkBrief servers. File exports and iOS share sheet actions are controlled by the destination app or service you choose.

Optional integrations

Notion is being introduced in selected TestFlight builds and is not generally released. Obsidian remains planned and is not yet available. Notion also requires the test service to be enabled. These integrations are optional: content is sent only when you choose to connect and export. Neither requires a new TalkBrief account.

The Notion section describes the implemented test service. The Obsidian section describes its planned data flow. Contact us using the address below with privacy questions or deletion requests.

Notion

The Notion connection uses Notion's official authorization page, where you sign in and choose the workspace and pages to share. TalkBrief will not receive your Notion password. Access will be limited by the permissions you grant, including access inherited by child pages. The integration uses read, insert, and update content permissions to support your exports; it does not request comment or personal profile permissions.

When you choose to send a brief, its title, date, duration, tags, summary, and action items pass through TalkBrief's Cloudflare service to your selected Notion page. A full transcript is included only if you turn it on; it is off by default. Audio is not included. TalkBrief processes this content for the requested export and does not store it long-term on the integration server.

The device connection credential stays in this device's iOS Keychain and is not synchronized to iCloud. The server stores encrypted Notion tokens and workspace information, a hash of the device credential, connection and export identifiers, content fingerprints, retry records, and rate-limit counters. These support authorization, opening and safely updating exports, and avoiding duplicates. There is no automatic or two-way synchronization.

Incomplete authorization expires after 10 minutes. Connections and export metadata expire after 180 days without successful authenticated use, at which point the service attempts to revoke access and clean up the records. Export updates affect only a previously verified TalkBrief-managed region; edited or unverifiable content requires your confirmation to create a separate copy.

Disconnecting in TalkBrief immediately disables the connection and removes export associations. TalkBrief attempts to revoke the Notion access token. If revocation fails, the encrypted token is retained only for cleanup retries. After successful revocation, a hashed retry record may remain for up to 24 hours before deletion. You can also remove the connection's access in Notion. If an interrupted authorization cannot be recovered, remove its access in Notion before reconnecting.

Disconnecting or revoking access does not delete exported pages. Deleting a TalkBrief recording does not automatically delete its Notion copy. Manage exported pages and their sharing permissions in Notion; their subsequent storage and use are subject to Notion's Privacy Policy.

Obsidian

The planned Obsidian integration creates Markdown notes in a vault or folder you choose, using a local app link or a Markdown file export. Notes may include a title, date, tags, source recording identifier, summary, action items, and a transcript if you choose to include it. The initial integration will not attach audio or provide two-way synchronization.

This local export is not intended to send your note content through TalkBrief's servers. If clipboard transfer is used for long notes, it will be explained before copying; copied text may also be accessible to other apps or devices through your clipboard settings. Obsidian Sync, iCloud Drive, or other services you choose may separately sync the exported files under their own settings and policies. See Obsidian's Privacy Policy.

Deleting the source in TalkBrief will not delete the exported Markdown note or its synced copies. Manage or delete those copies in your vault and any sync service you use.

Your control

You can delete recordings, transcripts, summaries, tags, and generated tasks from the app. You can also stop iCloud sharing, delete TalkBrief cloud data from your private iCloud, and choose whether to include transcripts when sharing a brief.

No sale of personal data

TalkBrief does not sell your recordings, transcripts, summaries, or Ask conversations. We use submitted content only to provide the feature you requested and to protect service reliability.
