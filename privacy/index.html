Privacy Policy

TalkBrief is designed around local recordings and clear AI requests.

Updated May 27, 2026.

Local transcription

Transcription runs locally on device when your iPhone supports TalkBrief's local transcription requirements. Your audio does not need to be uploaded for local transcript generation.

Cloud AI features

Summaries and Ask use cloud AI. When you request these features, TalkBrief sends the relevant transcript, summary, question, and selected source snippets to our AI service provider so it can generate a response.

Recordings and files

Recordings, imported audio, transcripts, summaries, tags, favorites, and task completion states are stored in the app's local container on your device unless you export or share them.

Subscriptions

TalkBrief uses Apple's in-app purchase system for subscriptions. Apple processes purchase and payment information. TalkBrief uses subscription status to unlock Pro features.

Your control

You can delete recordings, transcripts, summaries, tags, and generated tasks from the app. Exports are controlled by the iOS share sheet and any destination app you choose.

No sale of personal data

TalkBrief does not sell your recordings, transcripts, summaries, or Ask conversations. We use submitted content only to provide the AI feature you requested and to protect service reliability.
