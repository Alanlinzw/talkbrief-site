This policy explains how TalkBrief handles your personal information. In short: your recordings and notes are stored on your devices and in your own iCloud, and belong to your TalkBrief account; our servers keep only what sign-in, subscriptions and usage limits need; cloud transcription and AI features process the relevant content temporarily, only when you use them.

1. Who we are

TalkBrief is operated by Lin Zhiwu (林志武), the controller of the personal information described here. Contact: talkbrief.ai@gmail.com. This policy covers the TalkBrief iPhone app, Apple Watch app, widgets and web viewer.

We do not use advertising identifiers, track you across apps, or collect location, contacts or photos. Microphone, Reminders and speech recognition permissions are requested only when you use the related feature.

4. Data ownership, signing out and deleting your account

  1. Your library and personal settings belong to your TalkBrief account and are stored separately per account on devices and in iCloud. Another account signed in on the same device cannot see your data.
  2. Signing out hides your data and keeps it; it returns when you sign in again. You can choose Sign Out and Remove from This iPhone to delete only this device's copy; the iCloud copy stays.
  3. Deleting your account (Profile › Account › Delete Account, confirmed with Apple): we revoke Sign in with Apple authorization (if linked), ask Google to revoke access (if linked), and delete all sign-in accounts of your TalkBrief account and its server-side profile; the app deletes all recordings, transcripts, summaries and notes of that account on the device and in iCloud.
  4. If iCloud is unavailable at that moment, the app finishes deleting the iCloud copy when iCloud becomes available. Your other devices delete their local copies the next time they go online; a device that never goes online keeps its copy until it does.
  5. Account deletion does not affect files you exported, content already sent to Notion, Slack, Discord, Google Docs, OneNote or Obsidian, or tasks added to Apple Reminders. iCloud share links created by the account stop working. Deleting your account does not cancel your App Store subscription.
  6. If you stop using Sign in with Apple for TalkBrief in Apple settings, we sign out all your devices; if your Apple Account is deleted, we delete the corresponding TalkBrief account.

6. International transfers

These providers may process information outside your country or region, including in the United States, Singapore and the People's Republic of China. We protect it with encryption in transit, data minimisation and contractual safeguards.

8. Security

Sign-in credentials stay in this device's Keychain without iCloud sync; all connections are encrypted; server requests verify both the account and the device; logs do not record tokens or recording content. The operator accesses account records only for support, security and legal obligations (never recording content, which our servers do not hold), and every access is logged. No system is perfectly secure; if a security incident occurs, we will notify you and report it as the law requires.

9. Your rights

  • Access and export: Account › Export All Data exports recordings, transcripts and summaries.
  • Correction: edit your display name on the Account page; edit or delete recording content in the app.
  • Deletion: see Section 4.
  • Withdrawing consent: delete your account, or simply do not use cloud transcription and AI features. Withdrawal does not affect processing before it.
  • Other requests or complaints: email talkbrief.ai@gmail.com; we reply within 15 business days. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

10. Children

TalkBrief is not intended for anyone under 16. If you believe we have processed a minor's information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Third-party integrations

When you connect Notion, Slack, Discord, Google Docs, OneNote or Obsidian, credentials stay in this device's Keychain and belong to your current TalkBrief account. What is sent and how it is kept is described in each integration's in-app privacy note and is subject to that provider's policies. When you disconnect or delete your account, we ask the provider to revoke access where it supports this.

12. Changes to this policy

We will tell you in the app about material changes and ask you to agree again. Previous versions are kept at https://www.talkbrief.app/support/.

4. 数据归属、退出登录与删除账号

  1. 录音库和个人设置归你的 TalkBrief 账号所有，在设备和 iCloud 中按账号分开存放。同一台设备上登录其他账号，看不到你的数据。
  2. 退出登录：数据被隐藏并保留，重新登录后恢复；可选“退出并从此 iPhone 移除”，仅删除本机副本，iCloud 副本保留。
  3. 删除账号：在“我的 › 账户 › 删除账号”操作，需再次验证身份。我们会撤销 Apple 登录授权（如已绑定）、请求撤销 Google 授权（如已绑定）、删除该账号的全部登录账户和服务器上的账号资料；App 会删除该账号在本机和 iCloud 中的全部录音、转写、摘要和笔记。
  4. 若删除时 iCloud 暂不可用，App 会在 iCloud 恢复后自动完成删除。该账号的其他设备在下次联网时会删除其本机副本；一直未联网的设备，副本会保留到其联网为止。
  5. 删除账号不影响：已导出的文件；已发送到 Notion、Slack、Discord、Google 文档、OneNote、Obsidian 的内容；已添加到 Apple 提醒事项的任务。该账号创建的 iCloud 共享链接将失效。删除账号不会取消 App Store 订阅。
  6. 如果你在 Apple 设置中停止对 TalkBrief 使用“通过 Apple 登录”，我们会退出你的所有设备；如果你的 Apple 账户被删除，我们会删除对应的 TalkBrief 账号。
